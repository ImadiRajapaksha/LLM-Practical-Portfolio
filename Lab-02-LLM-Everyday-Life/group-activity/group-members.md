## My Contribution

My main tasks were writing the LLM prompts and creating the presentation slides.

For the prompts, I wrote and ran the prompts for each step of the activity: problem research, idea generation, idea critique, persona and naming. I gave the LLM a role and clear context, for example asking it to act as a UX researcher for university students in Sri Lanka, and I asked it to explain the role of AI and the feasibility of each product idea. After each answer, we discussed whether it made sense for students like us before I moved on to the next prompt. For example, the critique of our chosen idea led us to start with quick logging, categories and the safe-to-spend amount, and to use voice logging and notification access instead of relying only on bank SMS.

For the presentation, I created the seven slides: the title slide, the problem, the target user, the product and its five features, the mock-up screens, how we used the LLM, and what the LLM's critique changed. I used the LLM chat screenshots and the mock-ups in the slides as evidence of our work.

Gimhani worked on the LLM research and the mock-up designs. We discussed the LLM's answers together and made the final decisions on the product as a pair, including combining the ideas into one app and choosing the name SpendSense.
